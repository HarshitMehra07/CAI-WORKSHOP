# Development Prompts Used for ARIA Website

## Prompt 1 — Product concept
> Design a startup concept for ARIA, a personal AI assistant, companion and eventual personal AI operating layer. ARIA should understand the user, remember important context, adapt communication naturally, work through text, voice, camera, screen, internet and computer interaction, proactively help when appropriate, and require permission before important actions.

## Prompt 2 — Landing-page strategy
> Turn the ARIA concept into a startup landing page that communicates the problem, solution, target users, differentiation, growth potential and product vision. The page should feel premium, futuristic, trustworthy and human rather than like a generic AI SaaS template.

## Prompt 3 — Visual design
> Create a dark, cinematic interface using a restrained purple/cyan light system, large Space Grotesk headlines, glass-like cards, subtle gradients, a central AI orb/avatar, strong whitespace, responsive layouts and premium micro-interactions. Avoid visual clutter and avoid making ARIA look like a generic robot.

## Prompt 4 — Animation
> Add tasteful motion: scroll-triggered reveal animations, a breathing AI orb, orbiting rings, voice waveform movement, hover elevation and subtle cursor interaction. Keep animations smooth and performance-conscious. Respect mobile layouts.

## Prompt 5 — Startup pitch
> Write a concise startup pitch covering: problem, solution, target customers, value/uniqueness, growth/impact, business model hypothesis, MVP and go-to-market. Be ambitious about the vision but realistic about what should be built first.

## Prompt 6 — Critical product constraint
> Do not describe ARIA as an autonomous system that takes over the user's life. Position autonomy as permissioned execution. Important external actions require explicit approval. Privacy, transparency and user control are core product principles.
