# ARIA — Startup Pitch

## One-line pitch
ARIA is a personal AI assistant and companion that builds an ongoing understanding of you, then uses that context to help you think, work and act across the devices and tools you already use.

## Problem
Most AI assistants are excellent at producing answers but weak at continuity. Users repeatedly re-explain goals, preferences and projects and switch between separate workflows for voice, text, research, files, screens and actions.

## Solution
ARIA is designed as a persistent personal intelligence layer combining purposeful long-term memory, context awareness, adaptive communication, multimodal interaction, proactive assistance and permission-first execution.

**Understand → Remember → Help → Act → Learn.**

## Target customers
- Students and learners
- Creators
- Developers and builders
- Ambitious professionals

Expansion: families, founders, knowledge workers and small teams.

## Differentiation
ARIA combines **continuity + multimodality + proactivity + trust**. The product becomes more useful as it learns a user's working context while maintaining human control over important actions.

## Business model hypothesis
Freemium → Pro subscription → Power tier for advanced interaction, integrations and higher limits.

## Growth and impact
Start with students, creators and developers who already feel the pain of fragmented AI workflows. Expand through visual demos, short-form content, community and referrals. Long term, ARIA can reduce repetitive work and make advanced AI more accessible.

## MVP
Conversation, user-controlled memory, profile/context, voice, web research, tasks/goals, proactive suggestions and an approval gate before external actions.

## Closing
ARIA starts as an assistant. The long-term opportunity is a personal AI operating layer that understands the person behind the prompt.

**Your context. Your AI. Your control.**
