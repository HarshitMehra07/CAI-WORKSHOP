# ARIA — Development Prompts

## Product concept
Design ARIA as a personal AI assistant, companion and eventual personal AI operating layer that understands the user, remembers important context, adapts communication, supports text/voice/camera/screen/internet/computer interaction, proactively helps, and requires permission before important actions.

## Landing page
Turn the concept into a premium startup landing page communicating the problem, solution, target users, differentiation, growth potential and vision. Avoid a generic AI SaaS look.

## Visual design
Use a dark cinematic interface, restrained purple/cyan accents, large Space Grotesk headlines, glass-like cards, an expressive AI orb, strong whitespace and responsive layouts.

## Animation
Add tasteful scroll reveals, a breathing orb, orbiting rings, voice-wave motion, hover elevation and subtle cursor interaction. Keep it smooth and mobile-friendly.

## Startup pitch
Cover problem, solution, target customers, uniqueness/value, growth/impact, business model hypothesis, MVP and go-to-market. Keep the vision ambitious but the MVP realistic.

## Product constraint
Do not position ARIA as an uncontrolled autonomous system. Important external actions require explicit user approval. Privacy, transparency and user control are core principles.
